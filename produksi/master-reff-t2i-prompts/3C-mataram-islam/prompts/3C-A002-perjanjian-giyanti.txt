Cinematic realistic macro photography of Perjanjian Giyanti (Treaty of Giyanti, 1755), 1755 AD, Giyanti, Central Java — treaty that split Mataram into Yogyakarta and Surakarta. A Dutch colonial treaty document on dark surface. Visible: Dutch text with Javanese and Malay translations. The VOC coat of arms at top. Signatures of VOC Governor-General and Javanese princes. The Treaty of Giyanti (13 February 1755) split the Mataram Sultanate into two: Yogyakarta (under Hamengkubuwana I) and Surakarta (under Pakubuwana III). The treaty ended decades of civil war but permanently weakened Mataram by dividing it. Approximately 40 cm tall, yellowed paper, wax seal.
