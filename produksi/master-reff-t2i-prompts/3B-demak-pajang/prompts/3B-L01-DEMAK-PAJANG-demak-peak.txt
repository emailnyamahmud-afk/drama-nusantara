Cinematic realistic documentary wide establishing shot of Demak sultanate at its peak era (pantai utara Jawa Tengah, abad 16 Masehi, peak era 1520-1546 M under Sultan Trenggana). Coastal Islamic maritime capital — talasokrasi port kingdom, NOT inland agrarian royal city. Wide harbor (pesisir berpasir) with dozens of trading vessels: Javanese jong (largest in Nusantara), Chinese junks (with bat-wing sails), Malay perahu, Indian dhows. Sultan's istana (palace) on slight elevation — modest wooden structure on stilts with atap tumpang (early Javanese-Islamic style, NOT dome, NOT Chinese pagoda), candi bentar gateway. Bustling pasar at waterfront with multiethnic traders: Jawa, Tionghoa Muslim (latar belakang campuran), Arab, Persia, Gujarat, Bengali. Komoditas: lada, cengkih, pala, sutra Tiongkok, tekstil India. Kepeng tembaga Cina (picis) sebagai alat tukar. Masjid Agung Demak (built 1466 M) prominently visible: atap tumpang 3 tingkat (Iman-Islam-Ihsan), 4 saka guru, Lawang Bledeg (Pintu Petir, red gateway with two-headed naga ornament), candra sengkala "Naga Mulat Salira Wani" = 1388 Saka = 1466 M. Wali Songo (Sunan Kalijaga, Sunan Kudus) gather at masjid. Alun-alun (ceremonial plaza) in front of masjid. Sawah extending to horizon (access to interior penghasil padi). Gunung Muria in far distance. No people in foreground focus, but distant figures of traders at port, santri at masjid, jamaah entering for prayer, Wali Songo gathering on verandah.

Setting: Demak — kerajaan Islam pertama di Jawa, episentrum Islamisasi Nusantara. Letak strategis di jalur perdagangan pantai utara Jawa + akses ke wilayah pedalaman penghasil padi. Talasokrasi dengan budaya Islam kosmopolit — pedagang Muslim mancanegara bertransaksi sekaligus mendorong Islamisasi. Prinsip egalitarianisme Islam (tak seperti kasta Hindu-Buddha) bentuk solidaritas pedagang Muslim. Tomé Pires (Suma Oriental ±1500 M): kekuatan nyata di Jawa bukan lagi raja Majapahit, melainkan penguasa lokal semi-otonom. Sultan Trenggana (1505-1518, 1521-1546 M) taklukkan Majapahit di Kediri 1527 M, ekspansi ke Jawa Timur-Barat, Palembang, Banjarmasin. Demak runtuh setelah perang saudara Arya Penangsang vs Jaka Tingkir 1549 M.

Lighting: warm 3500K late afternoon golden hour sun from camera right (key light, long golden shadows on atap tumpang tiers, harbor, masjid), cool 6500K sky fill, mid contrast 3:1, sea mist.

Mood: Islamic maritime capital at its zenith — first great Muslim sultanate of Java, where Wali Songo gathered, where Majapahit was avenged, where Islam became the dominant political identity of Jawa.

Style: photorealistic JAVANESE wood (teak), palm fiber, brick, ceramic tile, batik, sail canvas, and tropical foliage textures, 35mm film grain, deep depth of field f/11, 24mm wide lens equivalent (masjid + istana + harbor prominent, NOT tiny), 8K hyperdetailed, color graded warm amber highlights cool blue sea shadows, BBC Earth documentary aesthetic.
