Cinematic realistic documentary wide establishing shot of Pajang kingdom (pedalaman Jawa Tengah, dekat Surakarta, ±1550-1582 Masehi, era Sultan Hadiwijaya). Inland agrarian Islamic kingdom — shift from coastal maritime Demak to inland agrarian Pajang. Wide landscape: Sultan's istana (palace) in center of valley, on slight elevation overlooking alun-alun (ceremonial plaza) and sawah (rice fields) extending to horizon. Istana is modest wooden structure on stilts with atap tumpang 3 tingkat (Hindu-Java meru form, NOT dome), candi bentar gateway, three pelataran (tribhuwana/triloka kosmologi). Pusaka mahkota Majapahit + harta kerajaan Demak dipindahkan ke Pajang (per Hadiwijaya) — symbol of inherited legitimacy from both Hindu Majapahit and Islamic Demak. Masjid Pajang nearby — atap tumpang (early Javanese-Islamic). Alun-alun with two waringin (banyan) trees. Punden berundak (stepped stone platform) for Sultan's appearances (sitinggil). Surroundings: sawah dengan irigasi tradisional, hutan jati di latar belakang, Gunung Lawu atau Merapi di kejauhan. Pujangga kraton (court poets) composing wayang Islam-Jawa with Punakawan sufistik (adaptasi Mahabharata-Ramayana). Aktivitas: petani di sawah, santri mengaji di langgar, abdi dalem di istana. Ricklefs: banyak bentuk ritus dan budaya istana Islam-Jawa lahir di era Pajang — kelak jadi ciri khas Mataram dan keraton penerusnya. Akulturasi: tasawuf Islam + mistisisme tantrik Buddhis. No people in foreground focus, but distant figures of abdi dalem, santri, petani.

Setting: Pajang — pindahan ibukota dari Demak (pesisir) ke Pajang (pedalaman dekat Surakarta) ±1549 M oleh Sultan Hadiwijaya (Jaka Tingkir). Pusat kerajaan agraris interior (perubahan orientasi dari maritim Demak). Pajang berusaha mengawinkan warisan politik Majapahit dengan tatanan Islam yang telah dibangun Demak (Sanusi Pane: menjembatani "kerajaan lama" dengan Islam). Era Pajang (1549-1582 M): hampir seluruh Jawa Tengah dan Timur di bawah satu payung. Akulturasi keagamaan mendalam: tasawuf Islam + mistisisme tantrik Buddhis; pujangga kraton adaptasi epos Hindu (Mahabharata, Ramayana) jadi wayang bernafaskan Islam dengan tokoh Punakawan sufistik. Hadiwijaya wafat 1582 M setelah kekalahan militer melawan Mataram; Pangeran Benowo serahkan kekuasaan ke Sutawijaya.

Lighting: warm 3500K mid-morning sun from camera right (key light, long shadows revealing istana atap tumpang and alun-alun), cool 6500K sky fill, mid contrast 3:1, mist from sawah.

Mood: inland Islamic agrarian kingdom — pivot point in Javanese Islam, where coastal maritime identity of Demak transformed into inland agrarian identity of Mataram to come. Where wayang with Punakawan sufistik was born, where the Majapahit pusaka found new Islamic home.

Style: photorealistic JAVANESE wood (teak), palm fiber, brick, batik, rice paddy, and tropical foliage textures, 35mm film grain, deep depth of field f/11, 24mm wide lens equivalent (istana + masjid + alun-alun prominent, NOT tiny), 8K hyperdetailed, color graded warm earth tones (brown wood, green sawah, blue sky), BBC Earth documentary aesthetic. Aspect ratio 9:16 portrait. High detail, 4K, sharp focus.

Avoid: people in foreground focus (small distant figures OK), dome on masjid (must be atap tumpang), tall minaret, Chinese pagoda, Majapahit kraton as dominant, Indian Hindu temple, modern buildings, modern Surakarta, anime style, cartoon, 3D render, watermark, text, signature, low resolution, blurry, oversaturated, 16mm ultra-wide distant shot.
