Cinematic realistic documentary extreme close-up macro of Wayang Islam-Jawa with Punakawan sufistik (Pajang era, abad 16 Masehi, ±1550-1580 M). Wayang kulit (leather shadow puppet) — traditional Javanese flat puppet made from buffalo hide (kulit kerbau) with intricate perforation (lubang-lubang) and painted details. CHARACTERISTIC FEATURE: this is the post-Islamization wayang — adapted by pujangga kraton Pajang (court poets in Sultan Hadiwijaya's era) from the Hindu Mahabharata and Ramayana epics into wayang bernafaskan Islam dengan tokoh Punakawan sufistik. KEY DIFFERENCES from Hindu wayang: ukiran wajah wayang menjadi mirip karikatur (caricature-styled, NOT realistic human face — reformation by Sunan Kalijaga era 3A, continued in Pajang); dewa Hindu names replaced with names of malaikat (angels) and nabi-nabi (prophets) per Islam; Punakawan (Semar, Gareng, Petruk, Bagong) — clown-servants with deep sufistik symbolism, representing spiritual truths. Lokasi: dalang (puppeteer) performing in pendopo kraton Pajang with blencong (oil lamp) casting shadows on kelir (white screen). Stick (gapit) attached to body, stem (cedhuk) for manipulation. Color: brown leather (kulit) with red, blue, gold pigment on painted details. Latar belakang: dim interior of pendopo with blencong flame visible, other wayang figures in soft focus. No people, no characters, no humans in foreground — but shadow of dalang's hand manipulating wayang is OK. Empty artifact reference (focus on the puppet).

Setting: Wayang Islam-Jawa dengan Punakawan sufistik — adaptasi pujangga kraton Pajang dari epos Hindu (Mahabharata, Ramayana) menjadi cerita wayang bernafaskan Islam. Tokoh Punakawan (Semar, Gareng, Petruk, Bagong) diberi nilai sufistik — simbolik untuk kebenaran spiritual Islam. Ricklefs: banyak bentuk ritus dan budaya istana Islam-Jawa lahir di era Pajang, kelak jadi ciri khas Mataram dan keraton penerusnya. Akulturasi Pajang: tasawuf Islam + mistisisme tantrik Buddhis. Sunan Kalijaga (era 3A, lanjutan ke Pajang) — pencipta wayang kulit dengan ukiran karikatur, tembang Lir-ilir, gamelan Sekati, batik surjan lurik, Grebeg Mulud, Sekaten. Pajang melanjutkan dan memperdalam sintesis ini.

Lighting: warm 3000K blencong (oil lamp) flickering key light from camera right (cast flickering warm glow on leather puppet, revealing perforation), low ambient fill, high contrast 5:1, dramatic puppet-show lighting.

Mood: wayang sebagai medium dakwah sufistik — where Hindu Mahabharata was reborn as Islamic sufi teaching, where Punakawan carried the secrets of tauhid in their caricatured forms, where shadows on the kelir became the threshold between zahir and batin, between the visible world and the spiritual realm.

Style: photorealistic kulit kerbau (buffalo leather, perforated, painted), wood (gapit, cedhuk), kain (kelir white screen), blencong oil lamp flame, 35mm film grain, shallow depth of field f/2.8, 100mm macro lens equivalent, 8K hyperdetailed, color graded warm amber leather + golden flame + cool blue shadow, museum specimen + wayang performance aesthetic. Aspect ratio 9:16 portrait. High detail, 4K, sharp focus.
