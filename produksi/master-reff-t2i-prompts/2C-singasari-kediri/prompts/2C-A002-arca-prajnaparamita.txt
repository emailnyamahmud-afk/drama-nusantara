Cinematic realistic macro photography of Arca Prajnaparamita (Singhasari masterpiece), 13th century AD, Singhasari — finest Buddhist sculpture from Java. A stone andesite statue of Prajnaparamita (Goddess of Transcendent Wisdom) on dark background. The figure sits in lotus position (dhyana asana) with hands in dharmachakra mudra (turning the wheel of Dharma). Serene face with half-closed eyes, elaborate jewelry (makuta, necklace, armlets). Andesite stone, grey-brown color with some weathering. Approximately 1.5 meters tall. One of the finest sculptures ever produced in Southeast Asia.

Lighting: warm 3000K raking light from camera left (key light, revealing stone texture and carving detail), low ambient fill. High contrast 4:1.

Mood: Perfection in stone — the Prajnaparamita of Singhasari, embodying the highest artistic achievement of East Javanese sculpture. This goddess of wisdom represents the intellectual sophistication of the Singhasari court, where Buddhist philosophy and artistic craft reached their pinnacle.
