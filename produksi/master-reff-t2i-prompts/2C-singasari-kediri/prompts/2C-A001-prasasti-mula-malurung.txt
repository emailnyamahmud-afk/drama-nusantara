Cinematic realistic macro photography of Prasasti Mula Malurung (first mention of 'Nusantara'), 1255 AD (1177 Saka), Singhasari — copper plate inscription with the word 'Nusantara'. A copper plate inscription (prasasti) — multiple plates joined by copper ring. Visible: Old Javanese script (Kawi/aksara Jawa Kuno) in Javanese Kuno language. The text contains the first written mention of 'Nusantara' — 'anuluyani nusantara' (then becoming Nusantara). Issued by King Wisnuwardhana. The plates show green patina, approximately 20 cm per plate. 5 plates visible.

Lighting: warm 3000K raking light from camera right (key light, revealing patina and script), low ambient fill. High contrast 5:1.

Mood: The first 'Nusantara' — the Mula Malurung inscription, where the word 'Nusantara' appears for the first time in history. Wisnuwardhana declared that the land beneath his feet would become the umbrella of the world, all Jawadwipamandala, then Nusantara. A concept born in 1255 AD that still defines Indonesia today.
