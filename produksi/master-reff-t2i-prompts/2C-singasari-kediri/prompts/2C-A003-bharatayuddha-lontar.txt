Cinematic realistic macro photography of Kakawin Bharatayuddha manuscript (lontar), 1157 AD, Kadiri — Mpu Sedah and Mpu Panuluh's epic poem. An opened lontar (palm-leaf) manuscript on dark surface. Visible: Old Javanese text in Kawi script, written with stylus on dried palm leaves. The text is the Bharatayuddha — the Javanese retelling of the Mahabharata's great war, composed by Mpu Sedah (started) and Mpu Panuluh (completed) in 1157 AD under King Jayabhaya. The lontar is aged brown, with some edge damage. Approximately 30 cm long per leaf, 4-5 leaves visible.

Lighting: warm 2700K raking light from camera left (key light, revealing script and palm-leaf texture), low ambient fill. High contrast 4:1.

Mood: Words that outlived kingdoms — the Bharatayuddha lontar, the greatest literary work of Kadiri. Written 1157 AD, it served as both epic literature and military manual. Its themes of dharma, duty, and righteous war resonated through Singhasari, Majapahit, and into modern Indonesian culture.
