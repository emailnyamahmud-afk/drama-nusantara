Cinematic realistic macro photography of Arca Harihara (Siwa-Wisnu deified form of Raden Wijaya), 13th-14th century AD, Candi Simping, Blitar — deified founder of Majapahit. A stone andesite statue of Harihara — a composite deity: right half is Siwa (with matted hair, third eye, trisula), left half is Wisnu (with crown, conch). Standing in samabhanga pose on a lotus base. The statue represents the deified form of Raden Wijaya (Kertarajasa Jayawardhana), founder of Majapahit. Stone is grey-brown andesite, weathered. Approximately 1.5 meters tall.

Lighting: warm 3000K raking light from camera right (key light, revealing the dual nature of the figure — Siwa and Wisnu), low ambient fill. High contrast 4:1.
