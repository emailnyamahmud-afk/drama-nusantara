Cinematic realistic macro photography of Sutasoma lontar (Mpu Tantular's Buddhist-Hindu synthesis), 14th century AD, Majapahit — the text that inspired 'Bhinneka Tunggal Ika'. An opened lontar manuscript on dark surface. Visible: Old Javanese text in Kawi script. The Sutasoma kakawin by Mpu Tantular — a Buddhist text that describes the unity of Siwa and Buddha. Contains the famous verse 'Bhinneka Tunggal Ika' — 'They are different, yet one' — which became Indonesia's national motto. The text embodies Majapahit's religious tolerance. Aged brown palm leaves. 4-5 leaves visible.

Lighting: warm 2700K raking light from camera right (key light, revealing script), low ambient fill. High contrast 4:1.

Mood: Unity in diversity — the Sutasoma lontar, where Mpu Tantular wrote 'Bhinneka Tunggal Ika, tan hana dharma mangrwa' — a phrase that would become Indonesia's national motto 600 years later. The idea that different paths can lead to the same truth — the spiritual foundation of tolerance in Majapahit and modern Indonesia.
