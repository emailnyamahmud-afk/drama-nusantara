Cinematic realistic macro photography of Nagarakretagama lontar (Mpu Prapanca's chronicle), 1365 AD, Majapahit — the most important historical text of Majapahit. An opened lontar (palm-leaf) manuscript on dark surface. Visible: Old Javanese text in Kawi script. The Nagarakretagama (Desawarnana) by Mpu Prapanca — a kakawin (poem) describing King Hayam Wuruk's royal progress through Majapahit's territories. Lists temples, provinces, customs, and political structure. The primary source for Majapahit history. Aged brown palm leaves, some edge damage. 5-6 leaves visible, each 30 cm long.

Lighting: warm 2700K raking light from camera left (key light, revealing script and palm texture), low ambient fill. High contrast 4:1.

Mood: The empire's memory — the Nagarakretagama lontar, without which Majapahit would be a shadow. Mpu Prapanca's poem is not just literature; it is a census, a map, a temple register, and a political manifesto. The document that proved Majapahit's extent and sophistication to the world.
