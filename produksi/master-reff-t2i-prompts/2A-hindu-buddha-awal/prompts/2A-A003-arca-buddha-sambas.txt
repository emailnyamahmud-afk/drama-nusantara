Cinematic realistic macro photography of Arca Buddha Sambas (gold and silver Buddha), 7th-8th century AD, Sambas, Kalimantan Barat — early Mahayana Buddhist art. 3-4 small Buddhist statues on dark velvet: 2 standing Buddha figures and 2 seated Bodhisattva figures, made of gold and silver. Each 10-15 cm tall. Post-Gupta style (Indian influence). Found at Sambas, Kalimantan Barat — evidence of early Buddhist presence. The 'Sambas Treasure' includes the finest early Buddhist metalwork in Indonesia. Gold figures gleam, silver figures tarnished.
