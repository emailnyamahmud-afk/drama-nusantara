Cinematic realistic macro photography of Prasasti Tugu (Purnawarman's canal inscription), 5th century AD, Jakarta — longest early Javanese inscription. A large round stone pillar (prasasti) with Pallawa script in Sanskrit, detailing King Purnawarman's construction of the Gomati canal. The inscription mentions: canal length 11 km (6,122 bows), 21 days construction, year 22 of Purnawarman's reign, 1,000 cows given to Brahmana. Stone is weathered with some erosion. ~1 meter tall, cylindrical.

Lighting: warm 3000K raking light from camera left (key light, revealing carved text), low ambient fill. High contrast 5:1.

Mood: Royal engineering — the Tugu inscription, recording the greatest public works project of early Java. An 11 km canal dug in 21 days, demonstrating the organizational power of Tarumanagara.

Style: photorealistic textures, 35mm film grain, shallow depth of field f/2.8, 100mm macro lens, 8K hyperdetailed, scientific documentation aesthetic. Aspect ratio 1:1 square. High detail, 4K, sharp focus.
