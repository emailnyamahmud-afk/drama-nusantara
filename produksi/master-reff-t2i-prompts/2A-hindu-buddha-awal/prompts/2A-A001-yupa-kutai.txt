Cinematic realistic macro photography of Prasasti Yupa (Yupa stone pillar of Kutai), 4th-5th century AD, Muara Kaman, Kutai, Kalimantan Timur. A single yupa (stone pillar) made of andesite, 1.5 meters tall, standing on a stone base. Visible: Pallawa script (Brahmi Akhir) carved in Sanskrit language, metrum Anusthubh poetry. Weathered surface with lichen. The 7 yupa at Muara Kaman are the oldest written inscriptions in Indonesia — recording Mulawarman's genealogy and 20,000 cow sacrifice.

Lighting: warm 3000K raking light from camera right (key light, revealing carved script), low ambient fill. High contrast 5:1.

Mood: First words — the Yupa of Kutai, the oldest written record in Indonesia. These stone pillars mark the moment Nusantara entered history: Sanskrit, Pallawa script, Vedic ritual, and royal genealogy, all carved in stone.
