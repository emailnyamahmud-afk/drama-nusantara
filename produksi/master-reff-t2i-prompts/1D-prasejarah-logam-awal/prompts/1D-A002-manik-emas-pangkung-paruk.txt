Cinematic realistic macro photography of gold burial beads from Pangkung Paruk, 122-331 AD, Pangkung Paruk, Bali — Roman/Indian gold beads. 10-15 gold beads arranged on dark velvet. Various shapes: spherical, barrel, bicone. Gold color with some oxidation. Each bead 5-10 mm.
