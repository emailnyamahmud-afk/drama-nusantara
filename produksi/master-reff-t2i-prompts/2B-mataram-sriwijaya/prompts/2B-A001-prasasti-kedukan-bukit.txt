Cinematic realistic macro photography of Prasasti Kedukan Bukit (Sriwijaya founding inscription), 682 AD (604 Saka), Palembang — oldest Sriwijaya inscription. A stone inscription (prasasti) on a small andesite stone. Visible: Pallawa Akhir script in Old Malay language, recording Dapunta Hyang's siddhayatra journey. The text begins 'Selamat tahun Saka 604...' The inscription is the founding document of Kedatuan Sriwijaya — the first written record of the word 'Sriwijaya'. Stone is weathered, approximately 30 cm tall.

Lighting: warm 3000K raking light from camera right (key light, revealing script), low ambient fill. High contrast 5:1.

Mood: Proklamasi — the Kedukan Bukit inscription, Sriwijaya's declaration of existence. The first time the name 'Sriwijaya' appears in history, recording Dapunta Hyang's sacred journey from Minanga to the Musi river mouth.
