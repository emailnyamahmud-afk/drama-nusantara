Cinematic realistic macro photography of Early Neolithic red-slipped pottery (Kalumpang style), 4,000-2,500 years ago, Kalumpang, Sulawesi Barat. 3-5 pottery sherds and one reconstructed vessel on dark surface. Red-slipped surface (distinctive Austronesian pottery tradition). Simple globular pot with everted rim, 15 cm tall. Decorated with incised geometric patterns (triangles, parallel lines). Burnished surface. The pottery tradition that marks the Austronesian expansion across Island Southeast Asia. Made by coiling and paddle-and-anvil technique.
