Cinematic realistic macro photography of Red ochre hand stencil (cave painting), 40,000+ years ago, Maros-Pangkep, Sulawesi Selatan. Close-up of a red ochre hand stencil on rough limestone cave wall. Hand placed flat against wall, ochre pigment blown around it leaving negative impression. Faded and mineral-coated with age. Multiple hand stencils visible in layers — different sizes (adults and children). The oldest known hand stencils in the world at ≥44,000 years. Cave wall texture rough, with natural cracks and calcite deposits.

Lighting: warm 2700K raking light from camera left (key light, revealing paint texture and cave wall topography), low ambient fill. High contrast 4:1.
