Cinematic realistic macro photography of pre-Austronesian rock art on limestone cave wall. Two motifs: (1) negative hand stencil (cap tangan negatif) - red ochre sprayed around an outstretched hand, leaving hand silhouette in natural rock color, fingers spread including thumb; (2) animal figure (babi / pig) painted in red ochre, crude but recognizable with body, four legs, head, and tail. Both motifs on rough grey-beige limestone with natural texture, cracks, and weathering. Pigment aged with reddish-brown patina. 40-20 thousand years old, Karst Maros-Pangkep or Sangkulirang-Mangkalihat. Lighting: warm 3000K raking side light from camera right (key light, revealing rock texture, hand stencil silhouette, pigment layering), low ambient fill. High contrast 5:1. Mood: hands across time, the earliest signature of human creativity - a hand pressed against stone 40 thousand years ago. Style: photorealistic limestone texture with visible mineral grains and weathering, 35mm film grain, shallow depth of field f/2.8, 100mm macro lens equivalent, 8K hyperdetailed, color graded warm earth tones (red ochre, cream limestone), scientific documentation aesthetic. Aspect ratio 9:16 portrait. High detail, 4K, sharp focus.
