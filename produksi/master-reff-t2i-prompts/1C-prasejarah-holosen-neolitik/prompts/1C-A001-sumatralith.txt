Cinematic realistic macro photography of Hoabinhian sumatralith (pebble tool), 10,000-5,000 years ago, Gua Harimau, Sumatra Selatan. 3-5 unifacial pebble tools (sumatraliths) on dark cave sediment surface. Large river cobbles (8-15 cm) flaked on one side only to create a working edge. Distinctive Hoabinhian tradition: thick, robust, versatile tools used for chopping, scraping, and cutting. Varied stone types: andesite, limestone, quartzite. Some show heavy use-wear and polished edges from prolonged use.

Lighting: warm 3000K raking light from camera right (key light, revealing flake scars and cobble texture), low ambient fill. High contrast 5:1.
