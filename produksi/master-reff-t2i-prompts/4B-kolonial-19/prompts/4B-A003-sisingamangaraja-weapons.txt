Cinematic realistic macro photography of Sisingamangaraja's spear and shield (Batak royal regalia), late 19th century AD (1877-1907), North Sumatra — royal weapons of the Batak king. A Batak spear (tombak) and wooden shield (perisai) on dark velvet. Visible: iron spearhead with intricate Batak carving on the shaft, wooden shield with traditional Batak geometric and figurative carvings (gorga motifs). The spear is approximately 1.5 meters long, the shield 60 cm. These are the weapons of Sisingamangaraja XII — the last Batak king who fought the Dutch for 30 years. The weapons are both practical tools and royal regalia — the spear carries the spiritual power of the Sisingamangaraja dynasty.
