A first edition of 'Max Havelaar' (1860) by Multatuli on dark surface. Visible: Dutch text, the title page with author name 'Multatuli', leather binding in dark brown, gold lettering on spine. The novel exposed the abuses of the Cultuurstelsel: forced cultivation, corruption, starvation in Lebak (Banten). It caused a scandal in the Netherlands and contributed to the system's abolition in 1870. Approximately 22 cm tall, 350 pages, aged but well-preserved.
