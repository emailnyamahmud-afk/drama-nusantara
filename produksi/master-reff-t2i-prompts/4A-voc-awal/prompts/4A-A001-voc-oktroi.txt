Cinematic realistic macro photography of VOC oktroi (charter of 1602, founding the Dutch East India Company), 1602 AD, Netherlands — the founding document of the VOC. A Dutch legal charter document on dark surface. Visible: elaborate Dutch calligraphy, the VOC monogram (Verenigde Oost-Indische Compagnie), the States General seal, and signatures of the Dutch States General. The charter granted the VOC a 21-year monopoly on Dutch trade east of the Cape of Good Hope, including the right to wage war, make treaties, and establish colonies. Approximately 40 cm tall, aged parchment with red wax seal.
