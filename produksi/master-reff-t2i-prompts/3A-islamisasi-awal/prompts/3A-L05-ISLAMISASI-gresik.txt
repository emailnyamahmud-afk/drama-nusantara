Cinematic realistic documentary wide establishing shot of Gresik, pesisir utara Jawa Timur, late 14th to early 16th century Masehi (peak era 1400-1500 M, era of Maulana Malik Ibrahim 1389-1419 M and Sunan Giri 1485-1505 M). Cosmopolitan Islamic port city of Majapahit era — richest trading port in Java per Tomé Pires. Wide harbor (pesisir berpasir, sandy coast) with dozens of trading vessels: Javanese jong (large), Chinese junks, Indian dhows, Malay perahu. Multiethnic traders on docks: Gujarat (largest foreign group per Pires), Kalikut, Bengal, Siam, Tiongkok, Maluku, Banda. Patih Yusuf (Malaka-born merchant, key figure of Gresik prosperity) directing porters. Salatin-wuzara-dawalah (sultan-menteri-government) — Muslim community institutions visible. Modest masjid at port edge (small prayer space, simple mihrab, no dome, no tall minaret — early phase). Pemukiman Tionghoa bagian tengah (from 1350-1400 M, central Chinese settlement). Maulana Malik Ibrahim's pesantren at Gapura (east of harbor, 1389 M establishment) — wooden langgar (musala) with adjacent asrama-like santri dwellings (adapted from Buddhist biara/asrama model). Masjid Pesucinan at Leran (1389 M, Maulana Malik Ibrahim's first foundation) — small wooden masjid with atap tumpang (early form). Makam Maulana Malik Ibrahim (1419 M) with nisan mirip Cambay (Gujarat) style — connection to Indian Muslim trade. Giri Kedaton visible on distant hill to east (Sunan Giri's domain, founded 1485 M). Sawah and coconut groves behind city. Gunung Penanggungan in far distance. No people in foreground focus, but distant figures of traders, santri, ulama.

Setting: Gresik — pesisir utara Jawa Timur, kota pelabuhan kosmopolitan sejak 1350-1400 M (pemukiman Tionghoa). Bandar dagang laut terkaya dan terpenting di Jawa per Tomé Pires. Patih Yusuf (lahir di Malaka) tokoh kunci keberhasilan dagang. Basis Maulana Malik Ibrahim (Sunan Gresik) sejak 1389 M, Sunan Giri (Giri Kedaton 1485 M). Komunitas Muslim disebut "salatin, wuzara, dawalah" (sultan, menteri, pemerintah) — sudah institusional abad 15 M. Hubungan dagang dengan Gujarat terbukti dari kemiripan nisan Maulana Malik Ibrahim dengan nisan Umar bin al-Kazurani (1333 M, Cambay).

Lighting: warm 3500K mid-afternoon sun from camera right (key light, long shadows on port and pesantren), cool 6500K sky fill, mid contrast 3:1, sea breeze.

Mood: cosmopolitan Islamic port city of Majapahit era — where Chinese, Indian, Arab, and Javanese worlds met; where Wali Songo began their dakwah; where the seeds of Javanese Islam were planted. A peaceful synthesis within Hindu Majapahit's tolerant umbrella.
