Cinematic realistic macro photography of Nisan Malik al-Saleh (oldest Islamic grave marker in Southeast Asia), 1297 AD, Samudra Pasai, North Sumatra — earliest Islamic tomb in Nusantara. A carved stone nisan (grave marker) made of Acehneses stone. Visible: Arabic calligraphy in relief, with ornate floral and geometric border patterns. The stone is approximately 1.5 meters tall, weathered grey-white. This is the tombstone of Sultan Malik al-Saleh, the first Muslim sultan in Southeast Asia, dated 1297 AD (696 AH). The stone shows a blend of Indian, Arabic, and local artistic traditions.

Lighting: warm 3000K raking light from camera right (key light, revealing carved calligraphy and stone texture), low ambient fill. High contrast 5:1.
