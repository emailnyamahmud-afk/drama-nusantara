Cinematic realistic documentary wide establishing shot of Ampeldenta, pesantren-pendopo complex east of Gresik (Surabaya region), early 15th century Masehi (era of Raden Rahmat / Sunan Ampel, 1401-1481 M). Small Islamic education compound within tolerant Majapahit territory — Raden Rahmat was appointed governor by the Hindu Majapahit king with full religious freedom. Layout: central langgar (musala, prayer hall) — modest wooden structure with atap tumpang (early Javanese-Islamic style adapted from Hindu-Java, NOT dome), open-air langgar with wooden floor raised on stilts, mihrab niche pointing west (kiblat), no minaret. Around langgar: rows of santri dwellings (asrama adapted from Buddhist biara model) — small wooden huts with ijuk roofs. Pendopo (open pavilion) for gatherings and instruction. Islamic instruction visible: santri (10-20 young men, distant figures) seated cross-legged around elderly ulama (Sunan Ampel) reading Qur'an from wooden rest (tilam), kitab kuning (yellow-covered Islamic texts) stacked. Method combines: ibadah (prayer), tablig (transmission), amal (practice). Pondok kitchen, well (sumur), garden with herbs. Boundary marked by low fence of bamboo. Beyond: sawah (rice fields) and coconut groves of Surabaya region. Masjid Jami Ampel (built 1421 M) nearby — wooden structure with 16 saka (pillars) berornamen kalimat syahadat (17 m tall), atap tajug 3 tingkat (adaptation of Hindu-Java meru), 5 gapura paduraksa (5 rukun Islam symbol). No people in foreground focus, but distant figures of santri studying, ulama teaching, families visiting.

Setting: Ampeldenta — pusat pendidikan Islam didirikan Raden Rahmat (Sunan Ampel) ±1401 M. Diangkat gubernur oleh raja Majapahit dengan kebebasan penuh dakwah. Method dakwah: maw'izah al-hasanah (nasihat baik) + wajadil hum bial-latihiya ahsan (dialog cara terbaik). Tarekat Naqsyabandiyah. Gelar susuhunan (sejajar guru suci Siwa-is yang berwenang diksha). Islamkan ±3000 KK. Mendidik Sunan Giri, Raden Patah, Sunan Bonang, Sunan Drajat. Masjid Jami Ampel 1421 M — 16 saka syahadat, atap tajug 3, 5 gapura paduraksa. Raden Rahmat wafat 1481 M, makam di kompleks Masjid Jami Ampel.

Lighting: warm 3500K morning light filtered through coconut palms from camera right (key light, dappled shadows on langgar floor revealing wood grain and santri activities), cool 6500K sky fill, low contrast 2:1, peaceful morning.

Mood: nascent Islamic educational institution — the cikal-bakal (proto-) pesantren, where Wali Songo's kader were trained. Adaptasi pendidikan biara Buddha menjadi pesantren Islam. Religious tolerance — a Muslim governor within Hindu Majapahit. Origin of systematic Islamic transmission in Java.
