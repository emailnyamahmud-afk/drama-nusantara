Cinematic realistic documentary wide establishing shot of Lobu Tua, ancient Muslim trading settlement at Barus, pantai barat Sumatra Utara, abad 9-12 Masehi (active 800-1100 M, before abandonment in 12th century). Multiethnic Muslim-Tamil-Jawa trading outpost, NOT a kingdom — a permanent settlement of Muslim merchants within a multiethnic port. Site in valley between two rivers (Aek Busuk and Aek Maca), 10 km from modern Barus. Earthen fortress (benteng tanah) perimeter: square shape, 375 m perimeter, 4-6 m wide, 3 m high — defense system. Inside fortress: rows of wooden stilt houses with palm-leaf (ijuk) roofs and bamboo walls (organic materials, no stone), simple masjid (small prayer space with mihrab), market stalls with kapur barus (camphor) blocks, kemenyan (frankincense), kayu gaharu, biji pala. Multitude of artifacts scattered: Arabic Glass fragments (Middle East Glass), glazed Islamic pottery (tempayan, pot, beads, bottles, plates, carafes), Khorasan glass stamp (10-11th century, "Demi Allah, Muhammad" inscription). Tamil inscription (946 Saka/1088 M, "Yang Ke Lima Ratus Dari Seribu Arah" by Tamil merchant guild, "Varocu" = Barus). Traders walking: Tamil (penguasa), Arab-Persia (Muslim), Javanese, lokal Batak. Sampan berlabuh di muara. Hutan kapur barus di latar belakang — pohon Dryobalanops aromatica (source of camphor). No people in foreground focus, but distant figures of traders at market, Muslim scholars near masjid, porters at river landing.

Setting: Lobu Tua — komunitas Muslim tertua yang terdokumentasi arkeologis di Nusantara sejak abad 9 M. >10.000 fragmen artefak Islam ditemukan: Arabic Glass, tembikar berglasir, manik-manik. Cap kaca Khorasan abad 10-11 M inskripsi "Demi Allah, Muhammad" — berfungsi sebagai hirz (jimat pelindung). Prasasti Tamil 1088 M menyebut "Varocu" = Barus. Multiethnic: Muslim Timur Tengah + Tamil (penguasa) + Jawa + lokal. Lobu Tua ditinggalkan abad 12 M (kemungkinan tsunami atau serangan Ayyavole India) → pindah ke Barus modern (Bukit Hasang).

Lighting: warm 3000K morning light filtered through tropical canopy from camera right (key light, dappled shadows revealing wooden structures and fortress texture), cool 6500K sky fill, high contrast 4:1, river mist.

Mood: ancient multiethnic trading outpost — the earliest Muslim community in Nusantara, where Arab-Persian merchants lived alongside Tamil and Javanese traders, where kapur barus (camphor) mentioned in al-Qur'an Surah al-Insan:5 was sourced. Modest, organic, before monumental Islamic architecture arrived.

Style: photorealistic wood, bamboo, palm fiber, earthen wall, ceramic, glass, and tropical foliage textures, 35mm film grain, deep depth of field f/11, 24mm wide lens equivalent (structures prominent, NOT tiny), 8K hyperdetailed, color graded warm earth tones (brown wood, green forest, grey fortress, blue river), National Geographic archaeological documentary aesthetic. Aspect ratio 9:16 portrait. High detail, 4K, sharp focus.
