Cinematic realistic documentary wide establishing shot of Giri Kedaton, complex on hilltop at Sidomukti, Kebomas, Gresik, late 15th century Masehi (founded 1485 M, Sunan Giri confirmed as ruler 9 March 1487 M, peak era until 1505 M). Dual-function institution: Islamic pesantren + political kingdom (dwitunggal — pandita ratu). Layout on hilltop: main kedaton (palace) at summit — wooden structure with atap tumpang 3 tingkat (Hindu-Java meru form, NOT dome), wooden saka pillars, candi bentar split gate entrance, ornamental naga undak (steps). Adjacent: pesantren buildings (rows of santri huts), langgar (musala), padepokan (study pavilions). Stone terraces descending the hill. Pond (kolam) for ablution. Makam Sunan Giri (wafat 1505 M, candrasengkala "Naga Loro Warnaning Padha" = 1428 Saka/1506 M) within complex — later addition by son Raden Supeno. Gapura candi bentar with hiasan undak berperipih arca naga. View from hilltop: Gresik port to west (with jong and junks at anchor, pesisir utara Jawa Timur), Madura Strait to north, Gunung Penanggungan to south, tropical forest covering hillside. Sunan Giri's dakwah network extended to Madura, Bawean, Nusa Tenggara, Ternate, Hitu (Ambon), Banjar, Kutai, Gowa — Giri was a major Islamic center that authorized kings of Demak, Pajang, and Mataram. No people in foreground focus, but distant figures of santri studying, abdi dalem (palace servants) at kedaton entrance.

Setting: Giri Kedaton — didirikan Sunan Giri 1485 M di atas bukit Sidomukti, Kebomas, Gresik. Dwitunggal: pusat politik kerajaan + pusat pendidikan Islam (pesantren). Dikukuhkan penguasa 9 Maret 1487 M oleh para wali + Raden Fatah. Lahirkan ulama penyebar Islam ke Madura, Bawean, NTT, Ternate, Hitu (Ambon), Banjar, Kutai, Gowa. Sunan Giri esahkan raja-raja Jawa Muslim (Demak, Pajang, Mataram). Sunan Giri wafat 1505 M, dimakamkan di lingkungan kedaton bersama putranya Raden Supeno.
