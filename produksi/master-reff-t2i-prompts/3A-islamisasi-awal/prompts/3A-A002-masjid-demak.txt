Cinematic realistic macro photography of Masjid Agung Demak (Javanese-style mosque architecture), 15th-16th century AD, Demak, Central Java — prototype of Javanese mosque. A detailed architectural model/fragment of the Masjid Agung Demak. Visible: the distinctive Javanese multi-tiered roof (tumpang tiga — three stacked roofs, NOT Arab dome), wooden pillars (saka guru — four main pillars), carved wooden mihrab (prayer niche), and decorative mustaka (finial) on roof peak. The mosque blends Islamic function with Javanese architectural tradition. 1:50 scale model. Wood and tile materials visible.

Lighting: warm 3000K raking light from camera left (key light, revealing wood grain and architectural detail), low ambient fill. High contrast 4:1.
