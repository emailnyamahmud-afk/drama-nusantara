Cinematic realistic documentary extreme close-up macro of Sakatatal — the legendary saka guru (main pillar) of Masjid Agung Demak, made from tatal (wood chips / wood shavings) bound together. Demak, Jawa Tengah, abad 15 Masehi (built 1466 M / 1 Dulkaidah 1420 H, per Babad Tanah Jawi). Created by Sunan Kalijaga when the fourth saka guru was not yet ready and the masjid was about to be erected — Sunan Kalijaga gathered serpihan kayu (wood chips from the other already-built saka guru), bound them together with rope, and formed a sturdy pillar. Pillar form: tall wooden saka (pillar), about 17 m tall (matching the 17 m saka of Masjid Agung Demak), diameter about 50-60 cm. COMPOSITION: NOT a single solid trunk — visible as bound bundle of tatal (wood chips / shavings / small pieces) held together by rope or rattan binding, with each chip aligned vertically. Wood: aged teak (jati), brown-grey with weathered patina. Carved ornaments: subtle Islamic geometric patterns on binding rings. Simbol: kesatuan dan semangat gotong royong — influenced by Al-Qur'an and hadis, also characteristic of Indonesian society. The other 3 sakaguru (by Sunan Bonang, Sunan Gunung Jati, Sunan Ampel) are smooth solid teak trunks — Sakatatal stands out as the chip-bound pillar, simbol of unity in diversity. Background: dim interior of Masjid Agung Demak, other saka visible in soft focus, rays of sunlight filtering through high windows. No people, no characters, no humans — empty artifact reference.

Setting: Sakatatal — saka guru timur laut Masjid Agung Demak, dibuat oleh Sunan Kalijaga dari tatal (pecahan kayu kecil-kecil) yang disatukan. Per Babad Tanah Jawi: saat tahap akhir membangun sakaguru, satu saka yang ditugaskan ke Kalijaga belum siap (Sunan Kalijaga sedang di Pamantingan). Setiba di Demak, masjid sudah akan didirikan tetapi satu sakaguru belum dibuat. Sunan Kalijaga mengumpulkan serpihan kayu sisa sakaguru yang sudah jadi, lalu mengikatnya menjadi satu — terbentuklah sakaguru yang siap didirikan. Pagi harinya, 1 Dulkaidah 1420 H, masjid dipercaya berdiri kokoh menghadap Ka'bah di Mekah. Empat sakaguru: Sunan Kalijaga (timur laut, Sakatatal), Sunan Bonang (barat laut), Sunan Gunung Jati (barat daya), Sunan Ampel (tenggara) — simbol empat pilar Islam: Al-Qur'an, hadis, ijmak ulama, qiyas.
