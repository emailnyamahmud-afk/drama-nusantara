Cinematic realistic macro photography of Koin emas dinasti Umayyah dan Abbasiyah (early Islamic coins in Nusantara), 7th-10th century AD, found at Bongal, North Sumatra — earliest Islamic artifacts. 5-7 gold and silver coins on dark velvet. Visible: Arabic calligraphy (Quranic verses) stamped on coins. Dinasti Umayyah (7th-8th century) and Abbasiyah (8th-10th century) coins. These coins were found at the Bongal site in North Sumatra, proving that Arab Muslim traders reached Nusantara as early as the 7th century — contemporaneous with the rise of Islam itself. Coins show wear from circulation. Each 1.5-2.5 cm.

Lighting: warm 2700K raking light from camera left (key light, revealing gold luster and Arabic calligraphy), low ambient fill. High contrast 4:1.
