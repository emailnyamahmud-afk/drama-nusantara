## Karakter Utama

### C001 — Panji Asmara Bangun (Inu Kertapati)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Nama** | Raden Panji Asmara Bangun |
| **Nama lain** | Inu Kertapati, Raden Panji Inu, Cekel Wanengpati, Kuda Wanengpati |
| **Peran** | Pangeran mahkota Kerajaan Jenggala, calon suami Candra Kirana |
| **Usia** | ± 25-28 tahun |
| **Lokasi** | Kahuripan (ibukota Jenggala), utara Sungai Brantas |
| **Sumber primer** | Siklus Panji — UNESCO Memory of the World (2017); Wikipedia *Panji tales*; Kakawin Smaradahana (Mpu Dharmaja) |

**Deskripsi fisik**:
- Pria Jawa, ± 28 tahun
- Wajah tampan, rahang tegas, mata tajam
- Kulit cokelat keemasan
- Rambut hitam panjang, sebagian terikat di atas (sanggul) dengan destar
- Kumis tipis, janggut pendek
- Tinggi ± 175 cm, postur tegap
- Biasanya digambarkan memakai cap/helm khas Panji (panji-cap, mirip topeng Malang)

**Kostum**:
- Destar/ikat kepala khas pangeran Jenggala (kadang dengan panji-cap bulat)
- Kain kamus (atasan) lengan panjang, warna biru tua/nila (warna Jenggala)
- Kain batik/gedog (corak parang, biru-emas) dari pinggang ke bawah
- Kalung emas sederhana (bukan sehebat raja)
- Keris pusaka di pinggang (gagang emas-kayu)
- Gelang kairo emas di lengan atas dan pergelangan
- Selendang emas-tipis di bahu

**Karakter**: Tegas, berani, cerdas, romantis. Bukan pangeran pasif — ia pejuang yang berpikir strategis. Pernikahan dengan Candra Kirana bukan kelemahan, tapi kebijaksanaan. Sosok yang menjadi prototipe dewa cinta Kamajaya dalam Smaradahana.

**T2I Base Prompt (Imagen 3 — format 5 komponen Google)**:
```
SUBJECT: Medium close-up portrait of a 28-year-old Javanese crown
prince, three-quarter view facing camera left, calm determined
expression with slight confident smile, long black hair partially
tied in sanggul topknot with blue-gold destar headwrap, thin mustache,
golden brown skin, sharp dark brown eyes. Wearing dark blue Javanese
prince tunic with gold thread embroidery (kala-makara motif on chest,
foliate scrolls on shoulders), blue-gold batik kain (parang rusak
pattern) from waist down, gold keyura armlet on left upper arm, gold
kankana bracelets on both wrists, kris pusaka with golden hilt at
left waist.

SETTING: Interior of Javanese Hindu-Buddhist imperial palace, East
Java 1100 CE, Prambanan/Penataran temple relief style architecture.
Stone andesite pillars with carved kala-makara, palm fiber (ijuk) roof
visible at top, gold mesh curtains blowing softly in wind, multiple
bronze torches along corridor in background.

LIGHTING: Warm 2700K torch light from camera right (key light, warm
amber glow on face), cool 5600K moonlight from window left (fill
light, blue rim on hair and shoulder), low ambient 400K.

MOOD: Tegas, berkuasa, slightly contemplative — a prince who carries
the weight of two kingdoms.

STYLE: Cinematic realistic portrait, Javanese classical aesthetic
inspired by Chinese period drama, shallow depth of field f/2.0,
50mm lens equivalent, 8K hyperdetailed, photorealistic skin texture,
35mm film grain, color graded teal-orange shadows warm highlights.
Aspect ratio 9:16.

Negative prompt: modern clothing, western architecture, Chinese temple
roof, Indian deity iconography, anime style, cartoon, 3D render,
plastic smooth skin, deformed hands, extra fingers, watermark, text,
signature, low resolution, blurry, oversaturated.
```

### C002 — Dewi Candra Kirana (Sekartaji)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Nama** | Dewi Candra Kirana |
| **Nama lain** | Sekartaji, Galuh Candra Kirana, Kuda Narawangsa (saat menyamar) |
| **Peran** | Putri mahkota Kerajaan Kediri/Daha, calon permaisuri Panji |
| **Usia** | ± 20-23 tahun |
| **Lokasi** | Dahanapura (ibukota Kediri), selatan Sungai Brantas |
| **Sumber primer** | Siklus Panji — UNESCO Memory of the World (2017); Wikipedia *Panji tales*; Kakawin Smaradahana (Mpu Dharmaja) |

**Deskripsi fisik**:
- Wanita Jawa, ± 22 tahun
- Wajah cantik klasik Jawa, mata besar ekspresif
- Kulit kuning langsat
- Rambut hitam panjang, diikat dengan hiasan bunga melati
- Tinggi ± 160 cm, postur anggun

**Kostum**:
- Kemben emas-brocade (atasan, gaya relief candi Prambanan/Penataran)
- Kain batik (corak parang klawu, merah tua-emas — warna Kediri)
- Mahkota emas kecil dengan hiasan bunga melati
- Kalung emas dengan liontin teratai (lotus)
- Gelang kairo emas di pergelangan tangan dan kaki
- Anting-anting emas
- Cincin emas

**Karakter**: Bukan putri yang pasif. Cerdas, punya pendapat, tahu nilai dirinya. Saat Panji datang melamar, ia tidak terima jadi "hadiah politik" — ia menuntut kepercayaan. Improvisasi dramatik yang membuatnya karakter modern, sesuai semangat siklus Panji (di episode *Panji Semirang*, ia juga aktif menyamar sebagai pria untuk melarikan diri).

**T2I Base Prompt (Imagen 3 — format 5 komponen Google)**:
```
SUBJECT: Medium close-up portrait of a 22-year-old Javanese crown
princess, three-quarter view facing camera right, serene yet firm
expression with subtle knowing smile, large expressive dark brown
eyes, fair golden skin, long black hair adorned with fresh jasmine
flowers (melati) and small gold ornaments. Wearing golden kemben
brocade top with lotus (teratai) motif embroidery, red-gold batik
kain (parang klawu pattern) from waist down, small golden crown
(makuta kecil) with melati flower ornaments, gold hara necklace
with lotus pendant, gold kankana bracelets on wrists and ankles,
gold kundala earrings.

SETTING: Javanese Hindu-Buddhist imperial palace garden, East Java
1100 CE, Prambanan/Penataran temple relief style. Background shows
carved stone pillars with foliate scrolls, lotus pond, frangipani
(kamboja) tree in soft focus, stone pathway with carved Panji
reliefs.

LIGHTING: Soft warm 3200K morning sunlight from camera left (key
light, golden glow on skin), cool 5600K sky fill from camera right
(soft blue ambient on hair), low contrast ratio 2:1 for feminine
soft portrait.

MOOD: Cerdas, anggun, dengan ketegasan tersembunyi — a princess
who knows her worth and demands to be seen as equal, not as gift.

STYLE: Cinematic realistic portrait, Javanese classical aesthetic
inspired by Chinese period drama heroines, shallow depth of field
f/1.8, 85mm lens equivalent for flattering compression, 8K
hyperdetailed, photorealistic skin texture with subtle pore detail,
35mm film grain, color graded warm golden highlights soft pink
shadows.

Aspect ratio 9:16.

Negative prompt: modern clothing, western architecture, Chinese
palace, Indian temple, anime style, cartoon, 3D render, plastic
smooth skin, deformed hands, extra fingers, heavy makeup, false
eyelashes, watermark, text, signature, low resolution, blurry,
oversaturated.
```

### C003 — Patih Jenggala

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Nama** | Patih (tidak tercatat nama historis spesifik) |
| **Peran** | Patih Kerajaan Jenggala, penasihat militer pangeran Panji |
| **Usia** | ± 50 tahun |

**Deskripsi**: Pria tua, jenggot putih, wajah penuh kerutan, mata bijak tapi waspada. Kostum sederhana — kain cokelat, kulit hitam (bukan emas), tanpa mahkota. Simbol loyalitas tetapi konservatif.

**Karakter**: Menentang pernikahan — takut Kediri berkhianat. Tapi patuh pada pangeran.

**T2I Base Prompt (Imagen 3 — format 5 komponen Google)**:
```
SUBJECT: Medium shot portrait of a 50-year-old Javanese patih
(minister), three-quarter view facing camera right, serious vigilant
expression with deep wrinkles around eyes and mouth, full white
beard, weathered dark brown skin, sharp dark eyes with crow's feet.
Wearing simple brown cotton kain (jedor pattern), dark brown leather
vest without ornament, plain cloth headwrap (blangkon style without
gold), simple leather sandals. No crown, no jewelry — symbol of
humble loyal servant. Hands clasped in front, slightly hunched
posture.

SETTING: Interior of Javanese Hindu-Buddhist imperial palace hall,
East Java 1100 CE. Background shows carved stone pillars with
kala-makara in soft focus, bronze torches flickering, blue-gold
Jenggala banner dimly visible on wall.

LIGHTING: Harsh warm 2700K torch light from camera left (key light,
strong shadows on right side of face — dramatic chiaroscuro), low
ambient fill. High contrast ratio 4:1 for weathered dramatic look.

MOOD: Wise, conservative, wary — a loyal advisor who fears betrayal
from old enemy.

STYLE: Cinematic realistic portrait, Javanese classical aesthetic,
shallow depth of field f/2.8, 50mm lens equivalent, 8K hyperdetailed,
photorealistic skin texture with deep pore detail, 35mm film grain,
color graded warm amber highlights deep brown shadows.

Aspect ratio 9:16.

Negative prompt: modern clothing, western architecture, Chinese
clothing, Indian ascetic, anime style, cartoon, 3D render, plastic
smooth skin, deformed hands, extra fingers, watermark, text,
signature, low resolution, blurry, oversaturated, gold jewelry on
subject.
```

### C004 — Mpu Dharmaja

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Nama** | Mpu Dharmaja |
| **Peran** | Pujangga kerajaan, penulis Kakawin Smaradahana |
| **Usia** | ± 40-50 tahun |
| **Sumber primer** | Kakawin Smaradahana (karya historisnya, era Kameswara r. 1182-1191 M) |

**Deskripsi**: Pria paruh baya, rambut diikat sanggul, janggut pendek, mata dalam (berpikir). Memakai jubah pendeta Siwa — putih-kuning, tasbih batu. Membawa lontar (manuskrip daun palem).

**Karakter**: Tenang, spiritual, pengamat. Ia yang merangkai kata-kata yang akan abadi. Saat membaca Smaradahana di upacara pernikahan Panji-Candra Kirana, suaranya bergetar — ia tahu sedang menciptakan sejarah.

**Catatan adaptasi**: Mpu Dharmaja secara historis adalah pujangga istana Kameswara (raja Kediri). Dalam adaptasi folklor Panji ini, ia dipindahkan menjadi pujangga yang menyusun Smaradahana untuk merayakan pernikahan Panji-Candra Kirana — masuk akal karena Smaradahana mengangkat pasangan pangeran-putri sebagai titisan Kama Jaya-Kamaratih.

**T2I Base Prompt (Imagen 3 — format 5 komponen Google)**:
```
SUBJECT: Medium shot portrait of a 45-year-old Javanese poet priest
(Mpu Dharmaja), three-quarter view facing camera left, contemplative
expression with slight serene smile, deep-set dark brown eyes with
subtle crow's feet, short gray-streaked beard, weathered golden brown
skin. Hair tied in sanggul topknot with simple white cloth band.
Wearing white-yellow Siwa priest robe (jubah) with thin gold-thread
border, stone bead rudraksha necklace, holding palm leaf manuscript
(lontar) with Javanese Kawi script in right hand, stylus in left hand.

SETTING: Javanese Hindu-Buddhist temple complex courtyard at night,
East Java 1100 CE. Background shows stone candi with carved reliefs
in soft focus, sacred fire (homa yajna) altar glowing, scattered
palm leaf manuscripts on stone table.

LIGHTING: Warm 2200K firelight from camera right (key light, intense
orange glow on right side of face and lontar), low ambient 500K
moonlight fill from camera left. High contrast 6:1 for sacred
dramatic chiaroscuro. Sparks from sacred fire floating in air.

MOOD: Tenang, spiritual, dengan beban sejarah — a poet who knows he
is writing eternal words.

STYLE: Cinematic realistic portrait, Javanese classical aesthetic
inspired by Chinese period drama, shallow depth of field f/2.0,
50mm lens equivalent, 8K hyperdetailed, photorealistic skin texture,
35mm film grain, color graded warm fire-orange highlights cool
blue-black shadows.

Aspect ratio 9:16.

Negative prompt: modern clothing, western architecture, Buddhist
monk, Christian priest, anime style, cartoon, 3D render, plastic
smooth skin, deformed hands, extra fingers, modern glasses,
watermark, text, signature, low resolution, blurry, oversaturated.
```
