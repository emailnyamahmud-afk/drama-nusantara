Glass stamp / seal (mohr) — small circular glass object, green bottle-glass color (kaca hijau botol), circa 3-4 cm diameter, abad 10-11 M. Origin: Khorasan, Iran (north-eastern Persia). Function: cap or seal (stempel) for stamping documents, wax, or fabric — also possibly a hirz (jimat pelindung / amulet) worn on body for protection in travel. In Persian Islamic tradition, such inscriptions appeared on objects of spiritual value.
