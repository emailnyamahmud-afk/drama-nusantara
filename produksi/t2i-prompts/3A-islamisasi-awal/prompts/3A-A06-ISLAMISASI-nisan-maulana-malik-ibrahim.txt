Carved stone nisan (tombstone) marking the grave of Maulana Malik Ibrahim — first of the Wali Songo to arrive in Java (±1389 M, died 1419 M). Nisan form: similar to Batu Aceh tradition — vertical headstone with ornamental top, but with Gujarat-influenced decorative elements.
