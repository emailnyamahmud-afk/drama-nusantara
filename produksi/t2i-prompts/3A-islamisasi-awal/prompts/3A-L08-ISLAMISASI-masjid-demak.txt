Cinematic realistic documentary wide establishing shot of Masjid Agung Demak, pantai utara Jawa Tengah, abad 15 Masehi (founded 1466 M / 1 Dulkaidah 1420 H per Babad Tanah Jawi, era of Raden Patah and Wali Songo). The first great mosque of Demak sultanate, built under guidance of Wali Songo, by Majapahit craftsmen. Layout: main prayer hall — large wooden structure on stilts with atap tumpang 3 tingkat (atap limas bersusun tiga, adaptation of Hindu-Java meru form, NOT dome, NOT Chinese pagoda). Empat sakaguru (four main pillars) at four corners — each made by a different wali: Sakatatal (timur laut, made by Sunan Kalijaga from tatal / wood chips); barat laut (Sunan Bonang); barat daya (Sunan Gunung Jati); tenggara (Sunan Ampel). Simbol empat pilar Islam: Al-Qur'an, hadis, ijmak ulama, qiyas. Pintu Bledeg — main gateway, red painted, ornamented with two-headed naga (Chinese-Java syncretic motif). Mihrab pointing kiblat to Mekah. Minimal exterior ornament — early Islamic aesthetic. Surroundings: alun-alun (ceremonial plaza) in front, keraton Demak to east (modest wooden palace of Raden Patah), pasar Demak nearby (port city), sawah to horizon. No people in foreground focus, but distant figures of santri studying, jamaah entering for prayer, Wali Songo gathering on verandah.

Setting: Masjid Agung Demak — didirikan 1466 M (1 Dulkaidah 1420 H) atas petunjuk Wali Songo, dikerjakan tukang-tukang dari Majapahit. Empat sakaguru (tiang utama) dibuat oleh empat wali: Sunan Kalijaga (timur laut — "Sakatatal" dari tatal kayu), Sunan Bonang (barat laut), Sunan Gunung Jati (barat daya), Sunan Ampel (tenggara). Simbol empat pilar Islam: Al-Qur'an, hadis, ijmak ulama, qiyas. Pintu Bledeg — gerbang merah berornamen dua kepala naga (sinkretisme Tionghoa-Jawa). Atap tumpang tiga (prisma + limas, adaptasi atap meru Hindu-Bali). Sakatatal = simbol kesatuan dan gotong royong. Raden Patah pendiri Demak diangkat oleh Wali Songo.

Lighting: warm 3500K late morning sun from camera right (key light, long shadows revealing atap tumpang tiers and Pintu Bledeg ornament), cool 6500K sky fill, mid contrast 3:1, mist from paddy fields.

Mood: first great mosque of Java — built by the unified Wali Songo at the dawn of Demak sultanate. Architectural syncretism: atap tumpang (Hindu-Java meru form) + sakaguru (ulama-cooperatively-built pillars) + Pintu Bledeg (Tionghoa-Jawa naga motif). Foundation of Zaman Kuwalen (era wali) replacing Zaman Budo (Hindu-Buddha).

Style: photorealistic JAVANESE wood (teak), palm fiber, brick, stone, and ceramic tile textures, 35mm film grain, deep depth of field f/11, 24mm wide lens equivalent (masjid prominent, NOT tiny), 8K hyperdetailed, color graded warm amber highlights cool blue shadows, BBC Earth documentary aesthetic. Aspect ratio 9:16 portrait. High detail, 4K, sharp focus.
