Cinematic realistic documentary wide establishing shot of Samudra Pasai kingdom, pantai utara Sumatra, late 13th to early 15th century Masehi (peak era 1300-1400 M). Coastal Islamic maritime capital — NOT Majapahit-style inland royal city, but a talasokrasi port kingdom. Wide harbor with dozens of trading vessels at anchor: Indian dhows with lateen sails, Chinese junks with bat-wing sails, Javanese jong, Malay perahu. Sultan's palace (istana) on slight hill overlooking harbor — modest wooden structure on stilts with palm-leaf (ijuk) roof, NOT grand Javanese tumpang-sari. Bustling pasar (market) along waterfront with stalls selling pepper (merica), camphor (kapur barus), cloves (cengkeh), nutmeg (pala). Crowds of multiethnic traders: Tamil, Bengali (largest group per Pires), Gujarati, Persian, Arab, Chinese, Malay, Javanese. Islamic features: small masjid with simple mihrab and stepped minaret, Muslim cemetery with carved stone nisan (Batu Aceh type with floral and calligraphic ornamentation). Royal cemetery with the famous nisan of Malik al-Saleh (1297 M). Mangrove-lined estuary. Coconut palms. Tropical haze. Ujung Sumatra in distance. Aromatic spice fragrances visible as golden dust in air. No people in foreground focus, but distant figures of traders in port, ulama near masjid, fishermen at harbor.

Setting: Samudra Pasai — first Islamic kingdom in Southeast Asia, founded late 13th M by Malik al-Saleh (formerly Merah Silu). Pantai utara Sumatra, controlled Selat Malaka trade. Tomé Pires in Suma Oriental described Pasai as rich and bustling. Bengal traders were the largest group. Strategic position between India-Tiongkok trade route.

Lighting: warm 3500K late morning sun from camera right (key light, long shadows revealing port and palace textures), cool 6500K sky fill from above, mid contrast 3:1, tropical haze.

Mood: cosmopolitan Islamic port kingdom — the first Muslim sultanate in Southeast Asia, where Arab, Persian, Indian, Chinese, and Malay worlds met. Strategic trading hub that integrated Islamic legitimacy with maritime commerce — Islamisasi + pembentukan negara + ekonomi berjalin erat.
