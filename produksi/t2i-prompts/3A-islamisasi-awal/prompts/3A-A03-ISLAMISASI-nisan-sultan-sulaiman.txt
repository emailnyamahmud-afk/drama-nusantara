Carved stone nisan (tombstone) marking the grave of a sultan of Lamuri kingdom at ujung utara Sumatra. Nisan tipe Batu Aceh (Acehnese tombstone tradition) — distinctive form: vertical headstone with "bersayap" (winged) shape at top, ornamented with intricate carved relief.
