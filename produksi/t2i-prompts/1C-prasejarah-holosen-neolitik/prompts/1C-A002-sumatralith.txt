Cinematic realistic macro photography of a Sumatralith (Hoabinhian pebble tool) on dark volcanic stone surface. Flattened river pebble (cobble) of andesite/basalt, roughly oval shape 12 cm long, with one face flaked to create a working edge (unifacial flaking). Other face left natural (cortex). Visible flake scars, sharp working edge, slight patina. Dark grey-black basalt with weathering. Hoabinhian culture, East Sumatra, 8 thousand years ago.

Lighting: warm 3000K raking side light from camera right (key light, revealing flake scars and patina), low ambient fill. High contrast 5:1. Dust particles in light beam.
