Cinematic realistic documentary landscape of bukit kerang (shell midden / kitchen midden) site, East Sumatra coast, 8 thousand years ago. Large pile of discarded shells (Gastropoda, Bivalvia) accumulated over centuries of coastal foraging, forming a small hill 3-5 meters tall. Coastal vegetation (pandanus, palm trees). Distant sea horizon. Stone tools and food remains scattered around midden. No main character in foreground (landscape only, tiny silhouettes for scale acceptable).

Lighting: warm 4000K mid-morning sun from camera right (key light, salt-air haze, golden on shells and sand), cool 6500K sky fill from above left. Mid contrast 3:1.

Mood: coastal abundance, the Hoabinhian foragers - centuries of coastal gathering accumulated into shell mounds.
