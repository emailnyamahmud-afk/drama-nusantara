Inside: human remains (bone fragments), a large bronze drum (50 cm diameter, used as burial container), gold ornaments (spiral, lempeng), and glass beads. Sarkofagus shows local carving style distinct from imported bronze drum. Color: grey andesite stone, green-patinized bronze, gold.
