Cinematic realistic macro photography of Tengkorak Trinil 2 (Trinil skull calotte), discovered 1891 by Eugéne Dubois, Trinil, Bengawan Solo, East Java — ~1.0 million years old. Skull cap (calotte) of Homo erectus on dark velvet background. Visible: thick cranial bone (10mm), low sloping forehead, prominent supraorbital torus (brow ridge), broad temporal fossae. Bone color: aged ivory with slight mineral staining (manganese dendrites). Post-mortem cracks filled with sediment. The specimen that Dubois named Pithecanthropus erectus in 1894 — the type specimen of Homo erectus. ~15 cm wide.

Lighting: warm 3200K raking light from camera left (key light, revealing bone texture and topography), cool 5500K fill from right. High contrast 4:1.
