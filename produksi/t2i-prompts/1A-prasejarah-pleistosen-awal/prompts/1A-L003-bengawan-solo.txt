Cinematic realistic documentary landscape of Lembah Bengawan Solo (paleo-Solo River valley), Pleistocene Awal, East Java, 1.8-0.9 million years ago. Wide muddy river meandering through open savanna landscape with gallery forest along banks. Stegodon herd (5-7 extinct elephants with downward-curving tusks) drinking at river edge, antelopes grazing on plain, deer in forest edge. Volcanic mountains on distant horizon (proto-Kelud, Kawi, Arjuno). No main character in foreground (landscape only, tiny silhouettes for scale acceptable).

Lighting: warm 4000K late afternoon sun from camera left low angle (key light, long shadows of animals and trees, golden amber on water surface), cool 6500K sky fill from above. High contrast 4:1. River reflecting sunlight. Mist on river.

Mood: primal ecosystem, predator and prey in balance - the cradle of human evolution in Java.
