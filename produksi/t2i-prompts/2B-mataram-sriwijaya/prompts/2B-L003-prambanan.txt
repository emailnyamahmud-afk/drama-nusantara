Cinematic realistic documentary landscape of Candi Prambanan (Siwagrha), the great Hindu Siwa temple complex, Prambanan valley, Jawa Tengah, 856 Masehi. Three towering stone temples: Candi Siwa (47m tall, center), Candi Brahma (south), Candi Wisnu (north). Volcanic andesite stone with intricate carvings: Ramayana relief on boundary walls, panteon astadikpalaka (guardian deities) in niches. 240 perwara (smaller) temples in courtyard. Tropical forest surrounding, Gunung Merapi in background. No modern elements. No main character in foreground (landscape only, tiny silhouettes for scale acceptable).

Lighting: warm 3500K golden hour sun from camera left (key light, long shadows of towers, golden amber on stone), cool 6500K sky fill from above. Mid contrast 3:1. Mist from Merapi.

Mood: Siwagrha — abode of Shiva, the grandest Hindu temple in Nusantara, built by Rakai Pikatan (Wangsa Sanjaya) in 856 M, standing alongside Buddhist Borobudur as proof of religious coexistence.

Style: photorealistic andesite stone textures (carved reliefs, towering spires, guardian statues), 35mm film grain, deep depth of field f/8, 24mm wide lens equivalent, 8K hyperdetailed, color graded warm earth tones (grey-brown stone, green forest), BBC Earth documentary aesthetic. Aspect ratio 9:16 portrait. High detail, 4K, sharp focus.
