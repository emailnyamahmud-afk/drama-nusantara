Cinematic realistic documentary landscape of Royal palace complex (keraton) of Mataram Kuno, Jawa Tengah, abad 8-9 Masehi (era Sanjaya-Syailendra). Grand royal complex in Jawa Tengah valley. Main pendopo agung with 16 carved teak pillars painted red-gold, peaked ijuk roof rising 15 meters, golden chandra-kapala (skull-crescent) ornament at peak. Flanked by dalem and pringgitan. Front gateway with stone dwarapala guardians. Ceremonial courtyard with stone prasasti. View to Gunung Merapi and Gunung Menoreh. High wooden palisade. Royal banner (red-gold) flying. Hindu Siwa + Buddhist Mahayana elements coexisting. No modern elements. No main character in foreground (landscape only, tiny silhouettes for scale acceptable).

Lighting: warm 3500K late afternoon sun from camera right (key light, golden on pendopo, stone guardians, and mountains), cool 6500K sky fill from above left. Mid contrast 3:1. Mist from Merapi.
