Cinematic realistic documentary interior of Pringgitan (Ceremonial Performance Platform) of Mataram Kuno, within royal palace complex, between Sindoro and Sumbing, abad 8-9 Masehi. Elevated wooden platform 20m x 15m, open-air with carved teak pillars supporting peaked ijuk roof. Used for wayang (shadow puppet) performances, gamelan concerts, and royal ceremonies. Stage with white screen (kelir) for wayang, bronze gamelan instruments (bonang, saron, gender, kendang) arranged on platform. Royal family seated on elevated platform opposite stage. Courtiers and dignitaries seated on lower level. Torches illuminating stage. View to both Mount Sindoro and Mount Sumbing from open sides.
