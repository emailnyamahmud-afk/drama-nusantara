Cinematic realistic documentary extreme close-up macro of Lawang Bledeg (Pintu Petir / Lightning Door), the iconic main gateway of Masjid Agung Demak (pantai utara Jawa Tengah, abad 15-16 Masehi, founded 1466 M / 1 Dulkaidah 1420 H). Massive wooden double-door gateway, painted red (warna merah) — distinctive red color dominates the gateway. Carved relief ornament on door panels: TWO-HEADED NAGA (two-headed serpent dragon) — Chinese-Javanese syncretic motif. CRITICAL FEATURE: ornamental kala (Hindu-Buddha demon face, simha-mukha / lion-head) — kepala raksasa di ambang pintu masuk, gabungan muka hewan + wajah manusia, kirttimukha. Para wali membuat bentuk dan pola penempatan kala yang disamarkan dari bentuk aslinya, mengikuti arus kuat wacana seni Hindu-Buddha — stiliasi (penggayaan), seni ornamen Islam Masa Peralihan. Candra sengkala relief dapat dibaca sebagai "Naga Mulat Salira Wani" = tahun 1388 Saka = 1466 M (887 H). Symbolism: relief merujuk Surah Al-Imran ayat 133-136 — simbol persatuan, toleransi, dan kerukunan beragama yang digambarkan melalui motif, pola, dan ragam hias. The door also functioned as the main gateway with two pucuk meriam perunggu (small bronze cannons) at sides in earlier eras. Wood: aged teak (jati) with weathered patina, traces of red pigment worn by centuries of monsoon rain. Metal hinges and locks. Background: dim interior of Masjid Agung Demak with saka guru (main pillars) blurred in soft focus. No people, no characters, no humans — empty artifact reference.

Setting: Lawang Bledeg (Pintu Petir) — pintu utama Masjid Agung Demak (1466 M, 1 Dulkaidah 1420 H). Candra sengkala "Naga Mulat Salira Wani" = 1388 Saka = 1466 M. Relief: dua kepala naga (Tionghoa-Jawa syncretic) + kala (kepala singa/raksasa Hindu-Buddha) yang telah distiliasi (disamarkan bentuk aslinya) — seni ornamen Islam Masa Peralihan. Simbol Surah Al-Imran 133-136 (persatuan, toleransi, kerukunan beragama). Warna merah dominan. Masjid Agung Demak dibangun oleh Wali Songo (Sunan Kalijaga + Sunan Bonang + Sunan Gunung Jati + Sunan Ampel buat 4 sakaguru), dikerjakan tukang-tukang dari Majapahit. Atap tumpang 3 (Iman-Islam-Ihsan). 4 saka guru (Sakatatal oleh Sunan Kalijaga dari tatal kayu).

Lighting: warm 3200K raking side light from camera right (key light, revealing wood grain, kala relief depth, naga ornament, red pigment patina), low ambient fill, high contrast 5:1, dramatic museum lighting.

Mood: the threshold of Javanese Islam — where devotees passed through a gateway carved with the synthesis of Hindu kala, Chinese naga, and Islamic calligraphic symbolism. Where the Prophet's lineage met the dragon of the East and the lion-guardian of Hindu temples, all sanctified by Surah Al-Imran.
