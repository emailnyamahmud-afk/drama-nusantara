Cinematic realistic documentary extreme close-up macro of Keramik Tionghoa on dinding sitinggil of Keraton Kasepuhan Cirebon (pesisir utara Jawa Barat, abad 16 Masehi, Keraton built 1529 M). Wall surface completely covered with antique Chinese ceramic tiles (keramik Tionghoa) — glazed ceramic plates, bowls, and tiles imported from various Chinese dynasties (Ming and earlier). Each ceramic piece displays distinctive Chinese motifs: NAGA (dragon, simbol kaisar Tiongkok, sinuous serpentine body with five claws); BURUNG PHOENIX (Chinese fenghuang, simbol abadi dari keanggunan, keabadian, dan harapan, with flowing tail feathers); GAMBAR IKAN (simbol kemakmuran, keberlimpahan, dan keberuntungan, often paired); also landscapes, floral, and calligraphic motifs. Glazed in cobalt blue, celadon green, iron red, and white — colors of Chinese export ceramics. Akulturasi Tionghoa-Cirebon: these Chinese motifs are embedded in a Javanese keraton structure (sitinggil elevated front pavilion) — the wall is part of a Javanese royal compound, not a Chinese imperial palace. Wood structural elements visible around ceramic wall — teak posts, atap tumpang above. Candi bentar gateway visible in background. Latar belakang: tropical foliage blurred.
