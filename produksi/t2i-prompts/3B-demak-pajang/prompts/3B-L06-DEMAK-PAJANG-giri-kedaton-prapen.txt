Cinematic realistic documentary wide establishing shot of Giri Kedaton at its peak era (Sidomukti, Kebomas, Gresik, Jawa Timur, late 16th century Masehi, era Sunan Prapen ±1548-1605 M). Largest Islamic education + political center in eastern Jawa — dwitunggal: pesantren + kerajaan (pandita ratu). Hilltop complex overlooking Gresik port: main kedaton (palace) at summit — wooden structure with atap tumpang 3 tingkat (Hindu-Java meru form, NOT dome), saka pillars, candi bentar split gate entrance with hiasan undak berperipih arca naga (naga ornament on steps). Adjacent: large pesantren buildings (rows of santri huts from various regions — Madura, Bawean, NTT, Maluku, Sulawesi, Kalimantan, Sumatra — all came to study), langgar (musala), padepokan (study pavilions), halaqah keilmuan. Stone terraces descending the hill. Pond (kolam) for ablution. Makam Sunan Giri (wafat 1505 M) within complex — candrasengkala "Naga Loro Warnaning Padha" (1428 Saka/1506 M). View from hilltop: Gresik port to west (with dozens of jong, junks, dhows — Tomé Pires described Gresik as "mutiara Jawa di antara bandar-bandar perdagangan"), Madura Strait to north, Gunung Penanggungan to south. Sunan Prapen's era (±1548-1605 M) — Giri Kedaton actively sent dakwah expeditions to Bali, Lombok (Bayan), Sumbawa, Bima, Madura, Bawean, Ternate, Hitu (Ambon), Banjar, Kutai, Gowa. Wayang Sasak media dakwah created. More than 1.000 keluarga perantau asing (termasuk Tionghoa Muslim) di Gresik awal abad 16 M. No people in foreground focus, but distant figures of santri studying in halaqah, abdi dalem at kedaton entrance, ulama in white robes.

Setting: Giri Kedaton — puncak di era Sunan Prapen ±1548-1605 M. Di atas bukit Sidomukti, Kebomas, Gresik. Dwitunggal: pesantren + kerajaan (pandita ratu). Babad Giri: Sunan Giri II utus ulama — Lembu Mangkurat ke Banjarmasin, Datu Bandan ke Sulawesi-Maluku (Makassar, Tidore, Seram, pulau Gala), Sunan Prapen ke Bali-Lombok-Sumbawa. Tomé Pires: Giri-Gresik sebagai "mutiara Jawa di antara bandar-bandar perdagangan" — pusat dagang + pusat Islam (priester-vorstendom). 1.000+ keluarga perantau asing (termasuk Tionghoa Muslim). Kurikulum pesantren: fikih (Syafi'i), tauhid (Asy'ari), tasawuf (Al-Ghazali), tafsir-hadis, ilmu dakwah, bahasa Arab — adaptasi lokal. Sunan Prapen dakwah ke Lombok ±1540-an, mendarat di Bayan (komunitas Muslim tertua Lombok). Media: wayang dengan kisah Amir Hamzah + Sayyidina Ali — cikal-bakal wayang Sasak. Warisan: tradisi Islam Wetu Telu di Lombok Utara.

Lighting: warm 3500K mid-morning sun from camera left (key light, long shadows revealing terraces, kedaton roofline, pesantren), cool 6500K sky fill, mid contrast 3:1, sea mist from Madura Strait.

Mood: dwitunggal Islamic center at its peak — where pesantren education trained ulama for the entire eastern Nusantara (Lombok, Sumbawa, Bima, Ternate, Gowa, Banjar), where a hilltop kingdom exercised both spiritual and political authority, where Sunan Prapen sent forth fleets of dakwah that reshaped the religious map of Indonesia Timur.
