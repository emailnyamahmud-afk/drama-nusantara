Cinematic realistic documentary landscape of the royal palace (keraton/istana) of Kan-to-li kingdom, hilir Sungai Batanghari, Jambi, abad 5-6 Masehi. Wooden palace on stilts with mixed Hindu-indigenous architectural elements — carved pillars with Ganesha and Wisnu motifs, peaked roof with palm fiber (ijuk), golden ornaments. Front courtyard opening to the river Batanghari, with busy trading port: perahu (boats) from India, Persia, and China (Tang dynasty) docking, merchants unloading goods (gold, camphor, frankincense, agarwood, damar resin, cinnamon). Market stalls with pottery, textiles, metal tools. Candi bata (brick temple, early Muarajambi) visible on elevation behind palace. Arca Nandi in small shrine. Royal banner flying. Tropical forest surrounding. No modern elements.

Setting: Royal palace of Kan-to-li, hilir Sungai Batanghari, Jambi, abad 5-6 Masehi. Center of maritime trade between India-Persia-China. Rulers: Varanarendra, Saba-la-na-lin-da. Controlled 2 ports: Fo-shi (Palembang) and Malayu (Jambi).

Lighting: warm 4000K late morning sun from camera left (key light, golden on palace, port, and river), cool 6500K sky fill from above right. Mid contrast 3:1. Sea haze, smoke from cooking fires.

Mood: cosmopolitan maritime kingdom, where the river meets the sea and trade flows — the palace of Kan-to-li, hub of the Indian Ocean-South China Sea trade network, where Hindu and Buddhist cultures met indigenous Sumatran traditions.
