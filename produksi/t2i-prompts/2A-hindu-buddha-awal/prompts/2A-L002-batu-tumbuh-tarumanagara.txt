Cinematic realistic documentary landscape of Batu Tumbuh, Tarumanagara capital (Tugu, Tanjung Priok, Jakarta Utara, abad 5 Masehi). Coastal settlement at edge of Teluk Jakarta, with wooden pier and trading boats. A long canal (Gomati, 9.2-11 km) recently dug, flowing from inland to the sea. Stone prasasti (inscription) with Pallawa script and pair of footprints (padavimbadvayam) of King Purnawarman. Wooden palace on stilts, tropical forest behind, distant mountains (Halimun). Trading boats from India and China at dock. No modern elements.

Setting: Batu Tumbuh, Tugu, Tanjung Priok, Jakarta Utara, abad 5 Masehi. Capital of Tarumanagara under King Purnawarman. Canal Gomati and Candrabhaga dug in 21 days (year 22 of reign). Prasasti Tugu, Ciaruten, Cidanghiang.

Lighting: warm 4000K morning sun from camera right (key light, raking across canal and prasasti revealing texture), cool 6500K sky fill from above left. Mid contrast 3:1. Sea breeze, mist over water.

Mood: thriving coastal kingdom, the first Hindu state in Java - where Purnawarman built canals for irrigation and left his footprint as Vishnu's mark on earth.

Style: photorealistic water, stone, and wood textures, prasasti with visible Pallawa script and footprints, 35mm film grain, deep depth of field f/8, 24mm wide lens equivalent, 8K hyperdetailed, color graded warm amber highlights cool blue shadows, National Geographic documentary aesthetic. Aspect ratio 9:16 portrait. High detail, 4K, sharp focus.
